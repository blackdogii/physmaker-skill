# Independent QA and repair

## Inspect every candidate

The reviewer must inspect the rendered artifact independently of the producer. Check:

1. Exact takeaway title and Traditional Chinese.
2. Names, dates, numbers, units, quotations, and citations.
3. Evidence boundary and absence of unsupported additions.
4. Arrow direction, sequence, topology, hierarchy, and labels.
5. Presence of every required key point.
6. Clipping, overlap, distortion, contrast, alignment, and safe margins.
7. Projection and thumbnail readability.
8. Match with the approved visual system.
9. Coverage of the speaker-note intent.

Correctness outranks beauty. Reject an attractive slide if it changes the research claim, reverses a process, invents classroom evidence, or loses an essential qualifier.

## Repair policy

- Use local repair for isolated text, spacing, alignment, masking, or label errors.
- Use the same renderer/backend and dimensions when repairing a raster slide.
- Regenerate when the composition, topology, or information hierarchy is fundamentally wrong.
- Reinspect every repaired output. A repair is a new candidate, not an automatic PASS.
- When comparing alternatives, rank factual and structural correctness before visual polish.

## Deck-level review

Render the entire deck and build a contact sheet. Inspect narrative continuity, rhythm, layout diversity, density transitions, terminology, visual drift, and closing strength. Then inspect full-size slides for details that thumbnails conceal.
