# Visual system and sample approval

## Visual-system specification

Record these decisions before full production:

```yaml
canvas: "16:9"
palette:
  background: "#0B1020"
  foreground: "#F4F7FF"
  accent: "#5EEAD4"
typography:
  display: "approved font"
  body: "approved font"
image_language: "editorial diagram | documentary photo | abstract technical"
icon_language: "outline | filled | none"
grid: "margins, columns, baseline"
density: "low | medium | high"
renderer: "tool and version"
backend: "model or rendering backend"
dimensions: "pixel dimensions when rasterized"
quality: "quality setting"
```

The values above are examples, not defaults. Use the audience, room, platform, and subject to choose the system.

## Functional layout variation

Maintain shared type, color, spacing, and image language while varying composition according to content. A process needs explicit direction; a comparison needs matched visual units; evidence needs a visible claim and boundary; a framework needs hierarchy.

Do not solve the deck with repeated card grids. Avoid using the same silhouette for three consecutive slides.

## Representative sample

Choose a content-rich slide that exposes typography, hierarchy, diagrams, labels, and density. A cover alone is not representative.

Approve the sample only when:

- all visible text is exact Traditional Chinese;
- the intended relationship is obvious without narration;
- the title and structure remain readable as a thumbnail;
- information density is credible and unclipped;
- imagery is relevant, licensed or generated appropriately, and not fake evidence;
- the system can support covers, processes, comparisons, evidence, and closing slides;
- renderer, backend, dimensions, and quality are recorded.

After approval, do not change the backend, model, dimensions, or quality casually. A material change requires a new representative review.
